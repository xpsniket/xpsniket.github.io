About

I’m Susobhan Pradhan, a developer who builds clean, practical web experiences and a photographer who chases abstract patterns, street moments, and landscapes. My work sits where logic meets light: structured code on one side, cinematic color and composition on the other.

By day, I write and maintain web interfaces with HTML, CSS, and growing experience in Python and C. Away from the screen, I photograph with an eye for mood, geometry, and story—then refine the results through Lightroom and a careful color-grading workflow.
